Publications

2024

Altered neuroepithelial morphogenesis and migration defects in iPSC-derived cerebral organoids and 2D neural stem cells in familial bipolar disorder

Phalnikar K, Srividya M, Mythri SV, Vasavi NS, Ganguly A, et al. (equal contribution among the first five authors)

Oxford Open Neuroscience, kvae007 · doi:10.1093/oons/kvae007

Organoids from patients in a clinically dense family had fewer neuroepithelial buds, a smaller ventricular zone and fewer cycling progenitors. Neural stem cells from patients migrated abnormally in time-lapse imaging.

2022

Gene Expression Analysis in Stem Cell-derived Cortical Neuronal Cultures Using Multi-well SYBR Green Quantitative PCR Arrays First author

Srinivasaraghavan VN, Zafar F, Schüle B

Bio-protocol 12(14): e4476 · doi:10.21769/BioProtoc.4476

A customizable, low-cost array of 27 markers and two housekeeping genes for screening how efficiently iPSCs become excitatory cortical neurons. Primer plates can be made in batches and stored at room temperature.

2022

Isogenic human SNCA gene dosage induced pluripotent stem cells to model Parkinson's disease

Zafar F, Srinivasaraghavan VN, Chen MY, Morato Torres CA, Schüle B

Stem Cell Research 60: 102733 · doi:10.1016/j.scr.2022.102733

CRISPR/Cas9 frameshift mutations in exon 2 of SNCA in patient iPSCs with an SNCA triplication produced a panel of clones for studying the effects of different alpha-synuclein levels.

Where next

Neurodevelopmental disorders: how genetic variants disrupt early brain development. I want to study this in human 3D models, organoids and assembloids, where the relationships between brain regions can be built and observed.

Timing between regions. The subplate holds back thalamic axons before they enter cortex, and the earliest cortical circuits run on a schedule that differs between species. I want to know whether that synchrony breaks down in autism-associated genetic backgrounds, and whether live imaging and single-cell tracking can measure it.

Critical windows. Testing when an exposure such as valproic acid matters during assembly of these circuits, using the same time-resolved profiling I built at Stanford.
